Pending sent is the count of outstanding invitations you've sent that haven't been accepted or withdrawn yet (LinkedIn → My Network → Manage invitations → Sent). Total connections is your live connection count; new connections is the day-over-day increase, used as a proxy for invitations accepted. Unread messages is your inbox unread count — a proxy for replies, not filtered to connection-request threads specifically, since LinkedIn doesn't expose that as a discrete number. Requests sent per week is shown two ways. When a week is marked (manual), the number is the actual send count Paul entered by hand — the source of truth, since LinkedIn doesn't expose a lifetime-sent figure. When marked (est.), no manual number was entered, so it falls back to a derived estimate: (change in pending invitations over the week) + (invitations accepted that week). A week runs Monday–Sunday. The current week is partial until Sunday closes it, so its % change is versus the prior complete week's total, not a like-for-like comparison, until the week ends.
